How we review your credit report

Upload your report. We review its readable information for reporting issues, probable violations and potential errors under relevant credit-report requirements. Choose the issues you want to dispute, review and edit your correspondence, then approve and download your packet to send to the bureau.

Readers support 5 validated presentations, including TransUnion Canada consumer disclosure and an Equifax Canada specimen, plus general credit-report intake. Checks use the information your report actually prints. Nothing is sent to a bureau automatically.
