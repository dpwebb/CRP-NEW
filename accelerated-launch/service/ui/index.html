Your guided workspace
Staging preview · test payments only. Use test cards and public samples, never real cards or private reports.
Supported reports and limits · general intake + 5 validated presentations · GB historical only

You can upload any plausible credit report that names a bureau and prints report-like content: it will be read where possible and its readable facts checked. Five presentations are validated more precisely: Equifax Canada (one pinned specimen), TransUnion Canada consumer disclosure, Equifax Australia, Experian United States, and Experian United Kingdom (a 2007 example, so present-day GB is not claimed). A document that is clearly not a credit report, or a report this build cannot read, is refused with a plain explanation — never silently accepted. Statutory comparisons run only on the specific presentation each is written for; a general upload still gets factual observations from the facts this build can read. Available checks depend on your selected region and are shown before purchase and upload.

Assessments are observations, not legal findings or bureau-response packets. No report is sent to an external AI or bureau. A purchase does not guarantee an issue will be found.
