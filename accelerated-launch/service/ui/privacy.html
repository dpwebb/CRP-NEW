Privacy and analytics choices

You can use Credit Regulator Pro without accepting Google Analytics.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics helps us understand visits to public pages. If you accept, it uses analytics cookies and receives page-view and technical information, such as your browser details and IP address. We do not use it inside your account or send your credit reports, identity documents or form entries.

This privacy page does not send analytics measurements to Google. Your choice applies to public pages on our live website and is saved in this browser for up to six months. Browser privacy controls may prevent measurement even after acceptance.

May we use optional Google Analytics on public pages?

Optional analytics stay off until you choose to accept.

These analytics choices do not change account security, document storage, billing or separate affiliate-referral choices. For privacy questions, contact admin@creditregulatorpro.com.
