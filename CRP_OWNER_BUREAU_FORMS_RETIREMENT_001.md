## Owner rationale

The owner reports that the research finds these standard formats preferred by bureaus but not mandatory. After reviewing CRP's packet output, the owner sees no benefit sufficient to justify the additional paperwork and delivery cost. This records the owner's product decision and research rationale; it does not create a new runtime legal finding or an independently verified universal legal assertion.

The consumer selects supported issues, reviews the correction/verification letter and selected source evidence and supporting document copies, approves the exact packet, and downloads it for consumer-controlled use. Preserve sourced recipient/contact details, relevant identification/address evidence requirements, source and account isolation, privacy, legitimate entitlement, correspondence editing, approval/version binding and usable PDF layout. Do not remove a relevant evidence obligation merely because it was documented alongside a bureau form.

Do not show form-only instructions, require form-only name/street boxes, attach blank or filled bureau templates, or direct the consumer to review/sign those retired forms. Printing instructions apply to the retained letter and packet contents. CRP does not sign, submit or mail on the consumer's behalf under this directive.
