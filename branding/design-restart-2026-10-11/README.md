# Credit Regulator Pro — identity restart studies

These are new concept studies, not adopted brand assets. Each mark is drawn in one color so its idea and small-size behavior can be judged before selecting a palette.

## 01 — Examined line

One record line is interrupted by a precise, open locator. It suggests examining a particular report fact, without implying that every report has an issue. Best fit for the evidence-first product. Risk: a tiny locator may disappear at favicon size; use the simplified symbol there.

## 02 — Open pathway

Two offset, open panels connect through a directional path. It suggests progress from report to consumer-approved packet. Risk: the arrow could suggest automatic sending; keep the path open and never depict a completed dispatch.

## 03 — Clear record

Three stacked record lines form a distinctive C-shaped field. It suggests organized, readable information and repeatable review. Risk: less specific to CRP; may resemble a generic document app.

## Evaluation

Test the three marks with people unfamiliar with CRP at 24, 48, and 160 pixels beside the full name. Ask unprompted what service they expect, whether they assume government or bureau affiliation, and whether they expect a guaranteed score or correction. Compare recognition after a short distraction and check contrast in one color. Select by accurate comprehension and recognition, then develop color and typography. Do trademark screening before adoption.
