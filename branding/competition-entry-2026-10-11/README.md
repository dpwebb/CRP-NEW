# Credit Regulator Pro — logo competition entry

## Concept

A custom **C/R monogram** gives the business a recognizable mark without borrowing the visual language of a credit bureau, court, or government agency. The C frames the R; the copper diagonal creates one deliberate point of attention. The wordmark stays calm and direct so the name remains legible in consumer and professional settings.

## Master files

- `credit-regulator-pro-primary.svg` — transparent horizontal logo
- `credit-regulator-pro-compact.svg` — two-line wordmark for narrower layouts
- `credit-regulator-pro-inverse-transparent.svg` — light logo for dark surfaces
- `credit-regulator-pro-one-color.svg` — single-ink use
- `credit-regulator-pro-symbol.svg` — standalone monogram
- `credit-regulator-pro-entry-board.pdf` — vector presentation sheet
- `credit-regulator-pro-entry-board.png` — raster presentation preview

All logo SVGs contain outlined letterforms and require no external font at display time. The source wordmark was drawn from Source Sans Pro and adjusted for the lockup.

## Palette

- Navy: `#142B3A`
- Copper: `#A57543`
- Warm neutral: `#F7F5EE`

Use the one-color version where color reproduction is limited. Reserve the standalone symbol for contexts where the full company name appears nearby or is already established.
