# BLOCKER-DISPUTE-PACKET-001 — consumer-ready dispute packets

Date: October 4, 2026 (America/Halifax). Authority: explicit owner instruction to include dispute-packet readiness as a production blocker.

Implementation: OPEN. Staging: PENDING. Production: BLOCKED.

This is separate from finding coverage and all-82 facilitation. An assessment report, demonstration download, draft endpoint or generic letter alone does not prove a dispute packet is ready.

## Required evidence criteria

- `packet_scope`: map the promised packet behavior and eligibility across all canonical 82 jurisdictions to accepted product and legal-source documents. Identify recipient types, required contents and jurisdiction-specific differences. Do not invent remedies, delivery obligations or unavailable addresses.
- `finding_evidence`: packet content binds to its own selected case, records and completed eligible findings; retains raw facts, locations, rule identity/version, relevant policy identity and VIOLATION versus PROBABLE_VIOLATION. Probable findings must not become categorical allegations. Unsupported checks and incomplete-findings explanations remain internal.
- `jurisdiction_content`: usable jurisdiction-appropriate dispute text and evidence references under supported duties; preserve source precision. Do not automatically treat an inconsistency or omission as a legal violation without its applicable duty. Do not weaken finding/packet permissions merely to enable generation; identify permission gaps for architect review.
- `consumer_review`: consumer can inspect and select the eligible matters included, supply necessary correspondence details, edit permitted personal/request wording and approve the packet. Those inputs are separately sourced and do not replace report facts. Do not invent a signature, approval or submitted status.
- `usable_download`: generate a coherent downloadable packet, including the applicable correspondence and organized evidence references or attachments required by the accepted packet specification. Test content and rendered layout where relevant; no raw debug dump or placeholder is considered ready. Ensure the output corresponds to the reviewed selection.
- `entitlement_isolation`: enforce legitimate download entitlements, account ownership and case association; no cross-account data, inaccessible private paths, provider keys or unrelated report content.
- `behavioral_coverage`: actual meaningful passing tests for promised jurisdiction/packet variants, positive and negative eligibility, edited/selected content, accurate classification, evidence association and downloads. Shared mechanisms may be reused with demonstrated jurisdiction applicability. Mark missing functional behavior OPEN.
- `end_to_end_journey`: real served-release review and purchased packet download verified separately. Pending hosted proof does not invalidate passing implementation tests, but cannot be relabelled successful.

## Workflow and closure

Include packet readiness in the current coverage inventory, alongside BLOCKER-FINDING-COVERAGE-001 and BLOCKER-ALL82-FACILITATION-001. First establish the accepted packet promise and all existing runtime generation paths. Preserve existing restrictions and identify what admitted source or explicit owner implementation decision is required to enable safe generation. Return a bounded recommendation for architect review before permission changes.

All materially promised packet functionality must exist and pass meaningful behavioral tests for implementation closure. Production remains blocked while applicable production requirements are unmet. No silent jurisdiction or packet-scope reduction is permitted. OWNER-CLOSURE-001 status separation remains in force.

External mailing, emailing, bureau submission, postage purchases or signing on a consumer's behalf are not authorized by this directive. Packet readiness means ready for the consumer's review and controlled use; additional delivery automation requires separate accepted scope and authorization.
