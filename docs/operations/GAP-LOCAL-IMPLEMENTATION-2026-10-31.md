## Evidence contracts and boundaries

- `reserve`: caller supplies the applicable documented reserve and working-size estimate. No universal reserve or expiry is invented; insufficient/invalid values refuse admission before side effects.
- `recoveryIndex`: accepts only matching app/build/generation receipts; manual success never becomes a scheduled witness. The existing October 31 JOBS acceptance proves its recorded monitors and five-file GCS staging readback, not every app's post-restore independent restore.
- `alertAcknowledgement`: relay acceptance alone is insufficient. Arrival and human acknowledgement need separate receipts tied to the incident and operator; no send occurs.
- `settlementCandidate`: normalized partner/destination/currency/amount/proposal/source bindings, independent review and human receipt are required. Synthetic evidence fails. Even a complete candidate returns settled:false/paymentExecution:false/providerConfigured:false. It is a review contract, not proof of bank authenticity or a BMO parser.
- `fulfillmentEvidence`: packet version/hash, authority and recipient review must match. Cancellation, duplicate dispatch or mismatched stage evidence blocks. Print does not imply mailing; mailing does not imply delivery; later stages require independently bound evidence. The helper neither executes nor grants dispatch.
