# Consumer workflow

[Knowledge base](../../KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md)

## From a report to an approved packet

1. Sign in and complete the account's required security steps.
2. Upload a supported report. Confirm the correct person, report date, country, region, and bureau. Unsupported or unreadable input needs a supported replacement; it must not be presented as a successful assessment.
3. Review the findings and their source evidence. A finding supports review and correspondence; it does not guarantee a legal conclusion, deletion, or bureau response.
4. Select the relevant findings and prepare the packet. Review every generated page, recipient, date, identification attachment, and edited letter.
5. Approve the exact packet version. Changed content requires the applicable review and approval again.
6. Download the approved PDF and keep the evidence. Customers may mail it themselves.
7. For the optional paid mailing service, review the exact quote and final printing copy, satisfy payment requirements, and explicitly authorize mailing. Follow order status inside CRP.

## Changes and deletion

Keep report identity and reporting dates attached to the correct saved record. A same-name upload is not necessarily the same report. Removing a report can affect downstream findings, comparisons, and letters that used it.

Read deletion and replacement dialogs carefully. Cancel preserves the current work. Confirm only the intended record and check which dependent work will change. Protect unrelated reports, edited letters, approved versions, and customer data.

## Limits and help

- A four-country homepage does not establish support for every bureau, report layout, or region. Use the exact accepted coverage cell.
- A subscription entitlement is not mailing permission. A successful payment is not proof of provider submission or delivery.
- Mailing or tracking evidence does not prove a bureau investigation or correction.
- Report a problem with the affected page, approximate time, and minimal sanitized context. Do not send passwords, authenticator values, recovery keys, or a full credit report through an ordinary support message.
