# Account details and bureau packet documents — October 8, 2026

Owner request: replace confusing signed-in authentication prompts with consumer account details, contact information and supporting-document uploads. Follow-up: choose the jurisdiction, then the bureau in Step 2. Batch 59 in the single work register owns this implementation.

## Consumer journey and custody

Signing in still opens Step 2. Returning to Step 1 displays the signed-in email, editable contact details, document upload/removal/download and sign-out. Provider configuration and build explanations were removed from this account panel. Contact details and supporting documents are account data, separate from extracted credit-report facts.

Step 2 offers country-specific bureaus for all 82 selections (Canada 13, US 57, UK 4, Australia 8). The selected bureau is saved on the case. Packet routing uses the selected issues' own report provenance; choosing a bureau cannot relabel report evidence. Mixed-bureau selections require separate packets. Account and public-record document requirements cannot be interchanged to bypass the relevant Canadian account checklist.

Consumers select the copies to include, review their correspondence and approve it. The downloadable ZIP contains the correspondence and exactly the selected original documents. Contact changes, selected-document changes/removal, changed issue selection and altered wording invalidate the reviewed approval. This feature prepares the consumer's submission; it does not verify identity or contact a bureau.

Uploads accept PDF, PNG and JPEG, up to 10 MiB each, eight account documents and the existing combined 40 MiB account storage limit. Opaque private blobs, account ownership, content/size validation, restart persistence, private inventory and deletion are reused. Removing a case leaves reusable account documents; removing a document or deleting the account removes its active stored bytes. Existing separate-backup and external-payment-provider deletion boundaries remain explicit.

## Resolved source differences and practical limits

- TransUnion Canada's current dispute page uses Hamilton; its linked PDF retains an older Burlington address. The current dispute page controls routing.
- Experian UK's correction-rights page identifies PO Box 9000. Addresses for report access or other complaints are not substituted.
- Equifax Australia's introductory text permits bank/utility documents within 12 months, while its utility table specifies paid bills less than six months old. The utility-specific rule is used; bank statements use 12 months.
- Australian point values use specific document classes. An unclassified birth certificate or generic government ID does not silently earn an Australian-specific value. Two distinct complete Canadian IDs may share a document category.
- Document kinds/dates and copy confirmations are consumer declarations. The application does not establish document authenticity, paid status, account/provider uniqueness or identity. Official instructions remain linked in preparation and the downloaded correspondence.
- No numeric age limit or universal ID count is invented where the ordinary dispute source does not state one. Bureau-specific follow-up evidence can be uploaded as supporting documents.
