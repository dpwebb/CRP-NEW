Facilitation here means the usable consumer journey: account access, supported report upload and assessment, at most two justified material questions when needed, applicable findings and supporting evidence, and access to the promised purchased output. Identify any additional promised packet/review/fulfillment behavior from accepted product documents; do not assume that external delivery or contacting third parties is included or authorized.

This is separate from BLOCKER-FINDING-COVERAGE-001. Finding coverage measures substantive rules and reachable finding capabilities. Facilitation coverage measures whether a consumer in each canonical jurisdiction can actually use the promised application journey. A jurisdiction selector, common infrastructure smoke test, mapping row, observation-only demonstration or generic unavailable screen does not prove the promise is fulfilled.
