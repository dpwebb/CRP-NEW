## Included minimum

1. Meaningful supported assessment in all 82 advertised jurisdictions; a selector is insufficient.
2. Ordinary supported PDFs, scans and image sets assessed with accurate record/bureau/report association.
3. Material supported reporting-issue coverage beyond retention, derived from the accepted jurisdiction/category inventory. No fixed quota of findings and no finding guaranteed on every report.
4. At most two proactively selected, governed material questions where genuinely needed; none to repair mandatory report omissions.
5. Clear reporting issues and probable reporting issues with exact supporting facts, applicable rule basis, source locations and appropriate precision.
6. Consumer-selected, reviewed and editable dispute/correction packets with organized evidence and usable downloads.
7. Continuing subscriber value: owned report history and comparison of subsequent reports, tracking whether previously identified issues remain, change or no longer appear. Absence in a later report alone does not prove a legal correction, deletion or bureau action.
8. Honest purchase/access state, cancellation, privacy inventory, deletion and usable support.

## Consumer presentation

Use plain, evidence-based issue wording rather than court-style legal conclusions. Preferred labels for existing supported classes:
- internal VIOLATION: Reporting issue, followed by the specific issue and supported rule basis.
- internal PROBABLE_VIOLATION: Probable reporting issue, preserving the specific evidence and material uncertainty of that completed issue.
- observation/consistency output: Report observation or Report inconsistency, kept distinct from a rule-backed reporting issue.

Do not present observations as proven violations, hide a probable classification, or weaken evidence gates. Internal classification and source identity/version remain accurate for audit and classification tests. Existing consumer-label tests must be updated to the approved presentation, not removed to hide failures.

No publicly surfaced legal-advice disclaimer or equivalent confidence-undermining boilerplate. State actual functions affirmatively. Incomplete-check explanations remain internal; actionable service errors remain available.

## Dispute and litigation boundary

The product organizes evidence and helps consumers request review/correction. Packets use factual statements and supported requests, not declarations that a court has adjudicated liability. Where a matter requires formal litigation, the product information remains supporting material for the consumer's consideration; it does not become a litigation service.

Excluded: court pleadings, lawsuit strategy, damages calculations, representation, guaranteed correction, signatures or approvals invented for the consumer, and automatic third-party dispatch. Do not claim that suing a bureau is always required to obtain changes. No public disclaimer is needed to describe these internal implementation boundaries.
