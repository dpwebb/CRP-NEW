# OWNER-B6-INGEST-002 — Flexible bureau-report ingestion

Date: 2026-10-01 (America/Halifax).
Authority: direct owner instruction in this conversation, following B6-INGEST-001.
Scope: consumer artifact admission, extraction-development evidence and ingestion UX. No runtime or existing governing file is changed by recording this instruction.

## Owner direction

Third-party credit-report examples, including test items, are acceptable examples of bureau-specific data points. Reports and image sets must not be rejected merely because their shape, size, pagination, channel variant or layout differs from a pinned specimen or known format family.

The ordinary admission standard is reasonable credit-report plausibility: the artifact identifies the credit bureau and contains report-like data points that reasonably support treating it as a credit report. Exact-byte identity, official publication, an independent second specimen or identical headings are not general upload prerequisites.

Clearly unrelated documents must receive a plain consumer message:

> This document or image set does not seem to be a credit report. Please upload an actual credit report issued by your credit bureau.

## Implementation interpretation for Cline

1. Accept third-party examples and test items for discovering field labels, bureau vocabulary, record structures, layout variation and extraction tests. Preserve their stated origin and sample/test status. Their use does not establish that the examples were issued by the bureau or belong to the uploading consumer.
2. Look for a bureau identity together with meaningful credit-report content, such as account histories, collections, balances, payment status, report dates or inquiries. A bureau name/logo alone is insufficient. Do not impose a universal mandatory field list or reject a short report merely because some sections are absent.
3. Accept unfamiliar plausible layouts for extraction. Record facts with their page/image and record locations. Existing family adapters are reusable extraction aids, not an exclusive admission whitelist.
4. Separate admission from reliable extraction and assessment. Partial reading must yield useful readable facts plus specific unreadable/uncertain fields. A missing or uncertain field blocks only the checks that require it. Never manufacture values or substitute one event date for another.
5. For unreadable images or ambiguous content, explain that the item could not be read or identified confidently and request a clearer copy. Do not call an unreadable credit report clearly unrelated.
6. Keep multiple bureau sections and individual account/collection records separate. Support image sets without borrowing dates between pages or records. Consumer jurisdiction remains explicit and is never inferred from the artifact.
7. Known samples/test items may be ingested and assessed as examples, visibly identified as such. They do not prove a consumer's actual reporting circumstances or authorize violation findings. An unlabeled plausible upload is not authenticated merely by passing admission.
8. Retain ordinary safe-file limits, account ownership, privacy and local/server extraction. This direction does not authorize external transmission of private reports, live billing or new legal finding classes.

## Changes to the prior Cline prompt

The B6-INGEST-001 requirements for an independent specimen or authoritative specification as a general family/admission prerequisite, and the blanket refusal of unsupported layouts, are replaced by this direction for ordinary consumer ingestion. Record the breadth actually exercised; avoid guarantees of perfect reading or every possible check.

Official, third-party, historical and test examples can all contribute data-point and extraction coverage, with honest labels. The absence of a current official GB layout is no longer a reason by itself to reject a plausible uploaded GB bureau report. Claims about demonstrated current GB extraction/assessment capability still require actual implementation evidence; do not turn a historical/test example into current official issuance evidence.

Existing exact-specimen legal-rule permissions, rule admission and finding restrictions remain separate. Broader ingestion does not automatically expand which legal checks may run or what conclusions they may emit.
