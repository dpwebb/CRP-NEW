## Distinct evidence thresholds

1. Definite reporting issue: retain the applicable rule's existing decisive evidence and exception requirements. Never promote an uncertain case to VIOLATION.
2. Probable reporting issue: affirmative report evidence supports a likely error or violation, with a specific material uncertainty. Document the inference and alternatives. Do not require every definite-finding predicate; do not fabricate a statistical probability.
3. Potential reporting issue: a concrete, source-linked discrepancy or supported reporting-error pattern warrants consumer verification even when likelihood is not established. Keep this distinct from PROBABLE_VIOLATION and VIOLATION. Missing data or suspicion alone is insufficient. A factual discrepancy need not prove a statutory breach to support a verification request.

For each potential/probable check, record the affirmative trigger, report/record association, source locations, inference, plausible benign explanation or refusal boundary, consumer wording, and factual verification/correction request. Preserve historical classifications. Introduce an explicit potential-issue representation if existing classes cannot express this honestly; do not silently relabel observations as legal findings.

## Consumer experience and packets

Show the supported issue, what the report actually says, why it merits attention, and the specific uncertainty in plain language. This uncertainty belongs to the surfaced issue; it is not an unresolved-check diagnostic card. Never claim that incomplete checks establish compliance. Do not assert a legal violation merely because a discrepancy is present.

Supported potential and probable issues may qualify for consumer-selected factual verification/correction packets. Certainty is not a universal packet prerequisite. Packet eligibility is issue-specific: source-linked affirmative basis, appropriate request wording, ownership, selection, review, permitted editing, approval of the current version, and entitled download. Do not automatically dispatch, invent approvals, assert proven liability, invent recipient addresses or remedies, or blanket-enable every adapter. Consumer choice controls pursuit; evidence controls the claim's wording and classification.
