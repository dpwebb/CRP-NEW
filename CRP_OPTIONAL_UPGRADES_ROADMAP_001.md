Owner version 2.0 direction: additions beyond the approved version 1 scope, including new jurisdictions, features, extra formats and extra check categories, are future-version candidates. They do not become current build prerequisites through ingestion or legal-corpus research. Preserve the existing 82-jurisdiction promise and complete unfinished core obligations before treating version 1 as ready.

Authority: OWNER-FINAL-RELEASE-SCOPE-001, October 4, 2026.

These are not initial-production blockers: automated delivery and provider integrations; proactive monitoring/reminders; advanced dashboards; extra personalization; additional languages/formats beyond explicitly supported scope; extra legally supported check categories beyond the approved core coverage matrix.

They have no assigned implementation deadline and no launch-blocking status. Add discoveries here rather than extending the required build. Existing core obligations must not be moved here simply because they are difficult or incomplete. Litigation services remain excluded.
