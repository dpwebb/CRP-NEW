## Mandatory customer outcomes

1. ALL82: functioning advertised assessment and facilitation across all 82 jurisdictions and supported formats.
2. INTAKE: reliable ordinary PDF/image-set reading, correct record/bureau/report associations, trusted decisive facts and appropriate date precision.
3. ISSUES: meaningful supported reporting-issue/probable-issue coverage, accurate rule/source evidence and at most two genuinely material questions. No arbitrary check quota, guaranteed finding or litigation conclusion.
4. PACKETS: consumer-selected, reviewed and editable correction/dispute packet and usable entitled download with jurisdiction-appropriate evidence. No automatic dispatch.
5. PURCHASE: one-time purchase delivers its configured case assessment and packet; subscriptions deliver their configured access, renewal/cancellation and accepted once-only upgrade credit. Preserve existing prices and accepted purchase entitlements; do not invent packages.
6. CONTINUING_VALUE: subscriptions additionally provide owned report history and subsequent-report comparison, with accurate issue-change tracking. The one-time purchase must not require a subscription for its promised completed output.
7. TRUST_AND_RELEASE: authentication, isolation, privacy/deletion, support, truthful product copy, durable storage, secret protection, legitimate billing verification and actual deployment provenance/authorization.

Consumer labels: Reporting issue / Probable reporting issue, retaining exact internal classes and evidence. One brief readable legal-advice disclaimer in the main-page footer only. No such disclaimer elsewhere and no incomplete-findings qualification cards.
