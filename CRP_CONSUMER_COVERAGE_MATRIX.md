Choose your current country and region, upload your credit report, read supported violations, choose what to dispute, review and approve your packet, then download and print it for mailing. ## Material repairs in this batch

- Own printed empty closed, first-delinquency and charge-off date captions now reach existing omission checks. Absent captions, unreadable values, N/A, dash placeholders, repeated captions and untrusted text do not prove omissions.
- Complete affirmative account status supplies the event; a leading fragment such as `Closed or Open`, `Closed: No` or an unconfirmed status cannot establish it.
- `Status: In Collection` remains attached to its current account instead of creating an anonymous collection. The account name, masked reference, status and date evidence remain together in findings and packets.
- Numeric own amount captions resolve usable records without an unrelated date prerequisite. An affirmative event plus its own printed blank can support an omission without an unrelated balance.
- GENERAL amount readings preserve their printed meaning. Credit limits, scheduled payments and past-due amounts do not become a generic amount owed or produce a false original-account/collection balance violation.
- Literal collection and write-off statuses are described as printed statuses. Results do not invent a code or claim payment-history evidence where the source is an account status.
- Checklist capability records respect collection record kind as well as required fields. A field list cannot imply that an unsupported dedicated collection detector ran.
- Source currency and current UK GENERAL support are stated separately. The coverage generator consumes complete source-matched execution instead of permanently recording implementation OPEN.
