How rewards work

1
Share your link

A customer chooses whether to visit CRP. Sharing or recruiting alone never creates a reward.

2
A customer buys

An eligible first purchase is verified. Rewards are calculated on the price before tax.

3
Three levels

The direct referrer earns 15%; the next two introducers earn 10% and 5% of that same purchase price.

4
Friday payments

After 14 days, 90% of each reward is scheduled for the next Friday. The remaining 10% is reserved for 180 days, then scheduled for a Friday.
