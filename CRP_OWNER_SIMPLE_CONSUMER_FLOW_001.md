# OWNER-SIMPLE-CONSUMER-FLOW-001 — prevent assessment and architect drift

Authority: the owner's explicit instruction in this conversation to correct unnecessary depth for future potential-violation assessment and confirmation. This is a standing product and build-review amendment.

## The consumer promise

Upload a report → review actual, probable or potential reporting issues → choose the issues to dispute → review and approve → download a packet the consumer can send to the credit bureau.

Keep this flow simple and factual. Preserve the Wizzard. Do not turn consumer results or correspondence into a legal research exercise.

## Consumer presentation

Each issue needs only the relevant printed facts, a short explanation of the concern, its honest confidence label, and a clear verification/correction request. State a material uncertainty briefly when necessary to avoid misleading the consumer. Do not display research limitations, source-retrieval caveats, edition investigations, internal failed checks, exhaustive exception analysis or hypothetical alternatives as routine consumer content. Keep those in internal records. Do not imply compliance when no issue is detected.

Supported uncertainty is a reason to use PROBABLE or POTENTIAL wording, not to suppress an otherwise useful issue or packet. Definite proof is required for a definite claim, not for every consumer verification request. Missing information alone is not affirmative evidence of an issue. Do not invent facts, legal authority or numerical probabilities.

## Standing architect and implementation rule

Before adding a gate, research task, qualification or follow-up correction, identify its concrete effect on the consumer outcome. Proceed only when it fixes an actual misleading statement, incorrect report association, unsupported legal attribution, duplicate issue, unusable packet, privacy/ownership problem or other demonstrated core defect. A possible nuance without a demonstrated effect is not a new build prerequisite.

Reuse accepted sources and completed tests. Record source quality accurately internally; do not relabel secondary material as an official capture. If a legal attribution genuinely remains unsupported, isolate that attribution and preserve the independently supported factual issue and verification packet. Do not require exhaustive legal confirmation to deliver that factual service.

Stop reopening a completed issue for progressively deeper research unless new evidence establishes a material defect. Complete independent useful work rather than repeatedly returning general inventories or proposals. Do not reduce the promised jurisdictions or core coverage.

## Ingestion, legal corpus and version 1 scope

Owner extension: apply the same anti-drift rule to ingestion procedures and the legal corpus. Expedite the current build; the owner may steer priorities or identify drift, but the architect remains responsible for applying these instructions without repeated reminders.

Ingestion: reuse existing readers and extraction paths. Read the facts needed by the approved checks, retain their report/account association and available provenance, and run independent supported checks even when unrelated fields are missing. Do not require a perfect extraction of the entire report before useful issues can reach the consumer. Correct actual misreadings and association defects; do not add universal format, completeness or hypothetical-edge-case prerequisites without a demonstrated core impact. Do not fabricate missing facts or relax ownership/privacy controls.

Legal corpus: reuse accepted relevant sources and mappings. Work on provisions needed for approved ordinary-report outcomes and existing jurisdiction promises. Do not restart exhaustive corpus inventories or repeated edition/source investigations without new evidence of a material attribution defect. Verify what is necessary for the claim made; isolate uncertain legal attribution while retaining independently supported factual verification. Keep research detail internal. Do not build disabled adapters merely to enlarge an inventory.

Version 1 remains the approved core service and its existing 82 jurisdictions. Additional jurisdictions, features, formats and check categories beyond that approved scope belong in `CRP_OPTIONAL_UPGRADES_ROADMAP_001.md` as version 2.0 candidates, not new launch prerequisites. Existing unfinished core intake, issue coverage, packets, privacy, billing and release requirements remain version 1 obligations.

Use related outcome batches, focused verification during development and one justified final regression for executable changes. Reuse valid completed evidence for unchanged code. Every review must either accept the completed consumer outcome or name a demonstrated defect, its consumer impact and the smallest fix; do not issue another general research proposal in place of ready implementation.

## Mandatory review question (applies to ingestion and corpus too)

Does this change make upload → understandable issue → consumer selection → usable packet more accurate or usable? If not, keep it out of the current core batch. Explain the concrete benefit of any additional requirement in one sentence.

This amendment supersedes conflicting instructions that add universal certainty gates or routine legal-analysis burdens to consumer results. It preserves truthful classifications, source-linked facts, selected-content approval binding, entitlement, privacy and account isolation. Implementation, staging and production remain separate statuses.

Documentation adoption establishes the standing instruction; it does not claim that all existing runtime wording already conforms. For documentation-only changes, verify the documents without rerunning the full assertion suite.
