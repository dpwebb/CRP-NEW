## 1. Purpose and authority

Deliver a paid, multi-user consumer wizard that works in all 82 enumerated jurisdictions at launch. Prioritize elapsed development time, reuse and a straightforward consumer experience. A jurisdiction selector or unavailable-result screen alone is not functioning coverage.

This plan constrains Codex and subsequent Cline instructions. It does not supersede existing governing documents by filename, immutability label or recency. Until activation, higher-ranked contracts and the active Cline order govern affected work. No file owned by that active order is changed by this draft.

PHASE5-001U is complete. Its separate admission record admits one exact-specimen CA-NS unit; the original rule-record bytes remain NOT_ADMITTED and no finding class is authorized. OWNER-ALL82-001 amends the prior plan's opening Scope and segment-completion sentence in quoted-replacement form, assigning this plan rank 5 for product implementation while leaving rule admission and output permissions intact. Preserve completed work, historic verdicts and legal-output restrictions. A claim of admission must be read from the actual admission record, not inferred from a passing test.

Owner-accepted legacy statutes remain accepted. Do not reopen missing provenance, past versions or prior-review evidence as a general prerequisite. A specific contradiction may require a scoped qualification or correction; it must not restart corpus-wide legal research.

## 2. Frozen launch scope

Enumeration: CRP-JURISDICTION-ENUM-1, 82 canonical regions: CA 13, US 57, GB 4, AU 8.

The launch includes:

- Consumer accounts, sign-in, paid entitlement and isolation between users.
- Explicit country and region selection before assessment.
- Private credit-report upload, supported-format detection and local/server extraction within the approved service boundary.
- Applicable accepted rules evaluated against reliably extracted report facts.
- Plain-language results, source locations and limitations.
- Consumer review and a downloadable response draft where supported; consumer controls fulfillment.
- A simple case record for the report, reviewed results, draft and consumer-recorded response status.
- Billing, operational recovery and a validated release to the selected host.

Multiple users, jurisdictions and bureau formats are required. At least one validated consumer-report format and one meaningful applicable check must work for each jurisdiction. The launch matrix must name the supported combinations. All bureau/format/check combinations advertised at launch must pass their own acceptance tests. No promise of every bureau, format or possible legal issue is implied; broader coverage requires an owner scope amendment.

National checks may serve multiple regions only through an explicit supported applicability relation. Merely changing the region label does not create region-specific statutory coverage. Any limitations in regional checks must be visible before purchase and upload.

## 3. Consumer promise

The consumer can select their jurisdiction, submit a supported report, receive a useful evidence-linked assessment, understand its limitations and prepare an appropriate next step without navigating internal governance terminology.

Useful results may be observations, explained comparisons or authorized legal findings. Qualified observations are acceptable; artificial certainty is not. Results must identify checks performed, unresolved checks and unsupported inputs. A supported report with no flagged issue may validly return an explained assessment; unconditional refusal does not count as functioning.

Never describe the product as checking all possible legal issues. Do not promise removals, score increases, guaranteed legal compliance or adjudicated violations.

## 4. Essential boundaries

- Jurisdiction comes from the consumer's explicit selection, never report address, filename, location or bureau.
- An anomaly or elapsed-period comparison alone is not a violation.
- Extraction failure is not proof that a field is absent.
- No consumer report or identifier is transmitted to an external AI/provider without separate authorization.
- Keep report data private; enforce ownership on every case, file and result operation.
- Preserve individual collection/tradeline identity; never borrow dates or values across records.
- Preserve unresolved timing, exceptions and output ceilings at the affected rule level.
- Current CA-NS support remains exact-specimen, observation-only and packet-ineligible until expressly changed. Ordinary upload admission uses reasonable credit-report plausibility — the artifact identifies a credit bureau and contains report-like data points — instead of a validated presentation boundary; measured extraction reliability and accurate coverage reporting are still required, and a bureau name alone is insufficient.
- No automatic bureau contact, mailing, legal filing or delivery claim. Downloaded drafts are not proof of sending.

## 5. Explicit exclusions

No CRM, marketplace, native mobile application, social features, general-purpose legal assistant, credit-score simulation, lender matching, automated litigation, automated mailing or parallel billing providers.

No broad rewrite of the legacy application. No edits to the read-only legacy checkout. No new parser, evaluator or framework when a suitable existing component can be reused. Do not copy a legacy component's credentials, production configuration or private fixtures into the new application.

No additional jurisdiction beyond the 82. No new legal research campaign, historic count reconstruction or per-source document series unless a concrete launch dependency requires it.

## 6. Build strategy and architecture limits

Reuse before writing code: catalog the existing rule definitions, evaluator primitives, parsers, tests, authentication and case-management behavior. Mark reuse candidates separately from verified components. Verify them in isolation before adoption.

Prefer one application, one local development service and one production deployment path. Use jurisdiction rule data and country/bureau format adapters around shared extraction and evaluation interfaces. Do not create 82 applications or duplicate an engine for every region.

Choose the service stack after the reuse audit; do not invent infrastructure to fill an unknown. Record the choice and reason once. Vendor provisioning and paid service engagement require the applicable authorization. Keep secrets out of source, logs and generated reports.

The consumer UI must not expose gate numbers, admission identifiers or internal classifications. Those remain implementation/audit data.

### 8.1 Mandatory production blockers

Owner amendment: OWNER-PRODUCTION-BLOCKERS-001, October 2, 2026. Both blockers below are **OPEN**. Production release is prohibited until both are implemented and verified on the release candidate. Development and staging validation may continue. These requirements are mandatory launch scope, not optional polish. Prior orders describing clarification as secondary do not exempt it from production acceptance.

**BLOCKER-FDT-001 — Failure-to-detect mitigation implemented and verified.** Implement the procedures in CRP_OWNER_FAILURE_TO_DETECT_MITIGATION_DIRECTION.md: equivalent documented labels/codes; structural and contextual reading; extraction-completeness checks; bounded local page/crop recovery; same-record corroboration with contradiction preservation; privacy-safe missed-detection diagnostics; and a representative benchmark measuring missed assessment-relevant facts and incorrectly confident readings by bureau/format/field. Preserve original bytes, raw values and source locations. No borrowed dates or unsupported semantic substitution. Unsuccessful keyword search is not proof of absence; parser failure cannot support a finding. A genuinely established absence must also meet the applicable rule's conditions before supporting breach. Acceptance requires baseline and post-change measurements, defined pass criteria recorded before evaluating the held-out examples, difficult/equivalent/context-negative cases, and deployed PDF/image-path evidence. A small synthetic benchmark must not be represented as universal accuracy.

**BLOCKER-CLARIFY-001 — Minimal optional consumer clarification implemented and verified.** Add a conditional plain-English "A quick clarification" step only when bounded automatic recovery cannot establish a simple fact that materially affects a useful assessment. Ask at most two questions per assessment; show an appropriate source excerpt for report-reading questions and briefly explain the benefit. Include "I don't know" and Skip, plus simple answers appropriate to the question. Do not ask legal interpretation, bureau-code interpretation or irrelevant field review. Skipping must leave independent checks available. Reliable reports must proceed without questions.

Distinguish (a) consumer-assisted transcription of report content, preserving the original extraction and source excerpt, and (b) optional off-report consumer assertions, preserving question, answer, source type and time separately. Neither becomes a bureau-reported fact merely because the consumer confirms it. A consumer statement that they initiated a dispute is not proof the bureau received required notice. Contradictory or unverifiable answers cannot automatically create or elevate findings. Existing report-fact, jurisdiction, classification and CA-NS permissions remain governing. Supplemental answers may inform qualified explanations; any expansion of their use as legal-finding evidence requires the exact higher-document amendment before that behavior is enabled.

Clarification acceptance requires deployed demonstrations of: no-question journey; one useful report-reading question; a separately labelled supplemental answer; "I don't know"; Skip with other checks continuing; contradiction handling; persisted correction provenance; and no unauthorized classification escalation. Consumer answers and excerpts must remain private and account-isolated.

**BLOCKER-COMMON-ERRORS-001 — Common credit-report error checks implemented and verified.** Status: **OPEN**. Owner amendment OWNER-COMMON-ERROR-CHECKS-001, October 2, 2026. The following are required launch capabilities, not optional enhancements or merely mapped subjects:

- Applicable retention and date-consistency checks, including legally relevant event dates and exceptions.
- Conflicting account status: paid/unpaid, open/closed, delinquency, settlement and responsibility statements associated with the same account and reporting period.
- Balance and amount inconsistencies: compare like-for-like current balance, amount past due and other established fields for the same obligation and time. Historical/high balances, different dates, transferred amounts and negative credit balances are not automatically errors.
- Potential duplicate reporting: establish account/obligation linkage and explain potential duplicated debt or incompatible entries. Original-creditor plus collection reporting, separate valid obligations, transfers and duplicate image pages must not automatically be called unlawful duplicates.
- Payment-history contradictions: compare reliably decoded history with status/date summaries for the same account and period. An unreadable grid cannot establish clean or incorrect history.
- Identity and account-responsibility inconsistencies: use report-internal identifiers and explicit ownership/role statements to surface supported inconsistencies. Address changes, aliases, joint accounts and authorized-user roles need appropriate handling. An unfamiliar creditor alone cannot prove identity theft or that an account is not the consumer's.
- Apparent re-aging and delinquency-date conflicts: preserve the event that actually starts the applicable reporting period; distinguish updates, transfers, placements, payments and later delinquency episodes. No reset or date borrowing. A proven date conflict may remain an observation if unlawful re-aging is not established.
- Applicable prohibited-content checks and required-content/qualification checks from accepted provisions. Evaluate actual jurisdiction-specific definitions, triggering duties, source context, cross-references and exceptions. Missing keywords are not established absence, and absent content alone is not breach without the applicable duty.

Use shared factual-consistency checks across the 82 regions where meaningful; apply statutory classifications only under the applicable accepted rule and existing permissions. Distinguish report-fact inconsistency, potentially incorrect reporting and authorized legal findings. Do not label every detected common error a violation or treat supporting source rows as independent checks. Exact-specimen-only behavior is not general-format completion.

Acceptance requires a category-to-executable-check matrix with required fields, source locations, record/time linkage, jurisdiction applicability, output permissions and deployed outcomes. For every category prove supported detection, legitimate lookalikes not falsely flagged, missing/contradictory facts withheld appropriately, and equivalent facts across supported layouts. Include end-to-end native-PDF and image/OCR tests for the shared extraction path. Cross-reference all 82 jurisdiction rows and advertised bureau/format combinations; do not equate a selectable jurisdiction with assessment coverage. Dependencies that genuinely cannot be established from a report must be named and qualified, with optional clarification used only under BLOCKER-CLARIFY-001. Do not invent external facts. A category cannot be marked complete from keywords, documentation or an unimplemented mapping; any material unsupported promised category keeps this blocker OPEN pending implementation or an explicit owner scope amendment.

**Release enforcement:** implement named checks for all three blocker IDs in the production release checker: BLOCKER-FDT-001, BLOCKER-CLARIFY-001 and BLOCKER-COMMON-ERRORS-001. Missing, stale, mismatched-build or failed acceptance evidence keeps the affected blocker OPEN and release not ready. Close only on implementation plus tested release-candidate evidence, recording build, tests, measurements and limitations in CRP_FINAL_ACCEPTANCE_REGISTER_PLAIN_ENGLISH.md. A document edit, question endpoint alone, upload acceptance or aggregate assertion count cannot clear any blocker. Revalidate affected evidence after subsequent relevant code changes.

Cross-cutting launch checks:

- User A cannot access user B's case, files, result or draft, including direct object requests.
- Wrong/missing jurisdiction and unsupported/malformed files produce clear refusals without fabricated analysis.
- Contradictory/ambiguous extracted facts restrict the affected check, not unrelated supported checks.
- Comparison results, observations and authorized findings remain distinct.
- Payment entitlement is verified by the service; redirects and client-side flags do not grant access.
- Report retention/deletion behavior matches the published policy; no report text enters application logs.
- The UI's availability and coverage wording matches actual tested support.
- A shared-code regression is exercised across affected country/configuration batches.
